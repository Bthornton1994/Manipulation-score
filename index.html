Our approach

Technology should give you
agency, not answers.

◌

Patterns, not verdicts

Language is complicated. We surface possible signals while leaving interpretation with you.

⌁

Privacy is the product

Analysis happens on your device. There is no account, upload, or message archive.

◇

Built for reflection

The goal isn't to win an argument. It's to create enough space to decide what feels right.

A thoughtful next step

A score is a starting point,
not the whole story.

Pay attention to patterns over time, how the interaction makes you feel, and whether your boundaries are respected. You do not need a high score to ask for space or support.
