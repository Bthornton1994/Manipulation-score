Media Lens

Media Lens looks at public articles, advertisements, speeches, and campaign material for evidence-linked language, claim, coverage, and source-context signals. It never scores a person or outlet, and it never produces a single 0-100 score for an article.

Before you analyze anything

Media Lens is for public material you are allowed to analyze, such as a published article, ad, speech, or campaign page. It is not the place for private messages or conversations; use Clarity for those instead. Clarity never sends your text anywhere.

Media Lens can run in fixture mode for local development, where pre-recorded example answers make no network calls. On the approved operator host, it can run a limited Jev-only live URL mode for public hosts on an operator allowlist. Live pasted-text analysis and the secondary classifier are disabled. Newsjack provenance data, when used, comes from files an operator already has; the worker never calls a Newsjack service.

Full article text is not stored by default. Nothing here is downloaded, uploaded, or exported unless you choose the export action after results appear.

Live URL access is limited, can be turned off by the operator, and is not production-ready for general use, a fact-checking service, or an accuracy claim. The disclosure below applies before you submit:

Before you analyze: the local worker will request the public page at the URL you enter. The destination site (and its CDN) will see this computer’s network address. Prepared public span text from that page may be sent to TypeSafe’s Jev classifier under TypeSafe’s own policy. TypeSafe does not fetch the URL. Full article text is not kept by Media Lens by default. Do not submit private messages, passwords, medical or financial records, information about children, paywalled content you are not authorized to fetch, or non-public/internal addresses. Live pasted-text analysis stays disabled — use Clarity for private messages.
