Limitations & Disclaimers

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Not a diagnosis

Clarity does not diagnose manipulation, abuse, mental health conditions, or anyone’s intent. A high or low score does not prove what someone meant or whether a relationship is safe.

Not a safety assessment

Clarity cannot determine whether you are in danger. If you feel unsafe, contact local emergency services or a crisis line—see Resources.

Pattern matching limits

Clarity uses a fixed set of language patterns. Many forms of pressure, coercion, or abuse may not be detected. Neutral or kind messages may still feel difficult. Context, history, and tone matter more than any text score.

Scores are not evidence

Analysis output is not suitable as evidence in legal, workplace, or disciplinary proceedings. Do not treat Clarity output as proof of wrongdoing or innocence.

Not professional advice

Response suggestions are starting points for reflection—not scripts from therapists, lawyers, or mediators. Adapt any language to your situation and relationships.

When to seek help

Consider speaking with a counselor, advocate, trusted friend, or support organization if communication consistently leaves you feeling afraid, controlled, or diminished—regardless of what Clarity detects.
