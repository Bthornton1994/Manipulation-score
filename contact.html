Contact and Corrections

Last updated: August 3, 2026

How to reach us

Email feedback@manipulationscore.com for privacy questions, accessibility feedback, incorrect or harmful output reports, correction requests, and general product feedback.

Email security@manipulationscore.com for security vulnerabilities. Do not post exploit details publicly before a fix is available.

You may also open a public bug report on GitHub for non-sensitive technical issues when the repository is accessible.

Response target: we aim to acknowledge feedback and security reports within five business days. Complex corrections may take longer; status updates are sent by email.

What not to send

  • Private messages you analyzed, paste only short, de-identified examples when necessary.
  • Names, phone numbers, addresses, or other identifying information about people in your messages.
  • Emergency situations, contact local emergency services or a crisis line instead.

For emergencies or immediate danger, call local emergency services. In the U.S., you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. See Resources on the homepage for additional crisis links.

Reporting incorrect output

When reporting a false positive or false negative:

  • Describe what Clarity showed (score, functions, or safety notice).
  • Explain what you expected and why.
  • If you include an example, use synthetic or heavily de-identified text.
  • Note the screening version shown in the footer (currently v0.3.3).

Disputed examples may be added to the regression test suite without publishing your original message.

Security reports

Email security@manipulationscore.com for security vulnerabilities. Do not post exploit details publicly before a fix is available.
