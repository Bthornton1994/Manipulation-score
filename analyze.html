How it works

From uncertainty
to understanding.

  1. 01

    Share the message

    Add the exact words that made you pause. Context belongs to you, and your text stays local.

  2. 02

    See the signals

    Review potential pressure patterns with plain-language explanations, not labels.

  3. 03

    Choose your response

    Use a grounded prompt to slow the conversation down and protect your boundaries.

Our approach

Agency,
not answers.

◌

Patterns, not verdicts

Language is complicated. We surface possible signals while leaving interpretation with you.

⌁

Privacy is the product

Analysis happens on your device. Optional local history stays on this device until you delete it, there is no account or cloud archive.

◇

Built for reflection

The goal isn't to win an argument. It's to create enough space to decide what feels right.

Resources

Support beyond
the screen.

Clarity is a reflection tool, not emergency support. If you need immediate help, use the resources below.

Immediate danger

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (911 in the U.S.).

Crisis support (U.S.)

Call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, free, confidential, 24/7. Learn more at 988lifeline.org.

Domestic violence support (U.S.)

National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 or visit thehotline.org.

Outside the U.S.

Contact local emergency services or search for crisis lines in your country or region.

These links are informational references. Clarity does not operate crisis services.
