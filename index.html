Our approach

Agency,
not answers.

◌

Patterns, not verdicts

We describe language functions in the text you supply. We do not label people, diagnose intent, or settle a relationship.

⌁

Privacy is the product

Analysis happens on your device. Optional local history stays here until you delete it—there is no account or cloud archive.

◇

Evidence before a score

A band is only a summary of matches you can inspect. Uncertainty stays visible. Regression tests are not real-world accuracy.

How Clarity works

From uncertainty
to understanding.

  1. 01

    Share the message

    Paste English words you are authorized to review. Context belongs to you, and the text stays local.

  2. 02

    See the signals

    Review possible pressure functions with highlights and plain-language explanations—not a character label.

  3. 03

    Choose your response

    Adapt a pause, boundary, or clarify prompt. The decision stays yours.

Clarity is a reflection tool—not emergency support. If you need immediate help, use the resources below.

Immediate danger

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (911 in the U.S.).

Crisis support (U.S.)

Call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Learn more at 988lifeline.org.

Domestic violence support (U.S.)

National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 or thehotline.org.

Outside the U.S.

Contact local emergency services or search for crisis lines in your country or region.

These links are informational. Clarity does not operate crisis services.
