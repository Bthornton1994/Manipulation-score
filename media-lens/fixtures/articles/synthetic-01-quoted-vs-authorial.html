Council approves downtown drainage upgrade

By Jordan Reyes

The city council voted on Tuesday to approve a $4 million drainage upgrade for the downtown district after three seasons of repeated flooding.

"We will fix the flooding problem this year," the mayor said during Tuesday's meeting.

The council said it would fix the flooding problem this year, directing staff to begin engineering work within 30 days.

Sources close to the situation report growing frustration among residents who have filed dozens of complaints this year.
