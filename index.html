The line we hold

Evidence for you.
Authority stays with you.

01

Patterns, not verdicts

We describe possible language functions in the text you supply. We do not label a person or infer intent.

02

Privacy is part of the product

Core analysis runs on your device. Optional history stays here until you delete it.

03

Uncertainty stays visible

A band summarizes screened patterns. It is not proof, diagnosis, or a safety assessment.

Support beyond the screen

If you feel unsafe,
get human help.

Clarity is a reflection tool, not emergency support. If you need immediate help, use the resources below.

01

Immediate danger

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the U.S., call 911.

02

Crisis support in the U.S.

Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Learn more at 988lifeline.org.

03

Domestic violence support

Call 1-800-799-7233 or visit thehotline.org.

04

Outside the U.S.

Contact local emergency services or search for crisis lines in your country or region.

These links are informational references. Clarity does not operate crisis services.
