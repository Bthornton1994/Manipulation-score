Contact and Corrections

Last updated: August 3, 2026

How to reach us

Open a GitHub issue on the public project repository: github.com/Bthornton1994/Manipulation-score/issues

Use this channel for privacy questions, accessibility feedback, incorrect or harmful output reports, correction requests, and security reports. For privacy-specific inquiries, include Privacy in the issue title.

Response target: we aim to acknowledge new issues within five business days. Complex corrections may take longer; status will be noted on the issue.

What not to send

  • Private messages you analyzed—paste only short, de-identified examples when necessary.
  • Names, phone numbers, addresses, or other identifying information about people in your messages.
  • Emergency situations—contact local emergency services or a crisis line instead.

For emergencies or immediate danger, call local emergency services. In the U.S., you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. See Resources on the homepage for additional crisis links.

Reporting incorrect output

When reporting a false positive or false negative:

  • Describe what Clarity showed (score, functions, or safety notice).
  • Explain what you expected and why.
  • If you include an example, use synthetic or heavily de-identified text.
  • Note the screening version shown in the footer (currently v0.2.8).

Disputed examples may be added to the regression test suite without publishing your original message.

Security reports

If you discover a security vulnerability in the site or application code, open a GitHub issue with Security in the title or use GitHub’s private vulnerability reporting if enabled on the repository. Do not post exploit details publicly before a fix is available.

Related policies

See Privacy, Acceptable Use, and Methodology for how data is handled and how Clarity should be used.
